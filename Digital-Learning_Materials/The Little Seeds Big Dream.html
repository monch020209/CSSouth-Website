The Little Seed’s Big Dream

In a quiet garden, a tiny seed rested under the soil. Around it, roots from tall plants stretched wide, and earthworms moved through the soft ground. The tiny seed dreamed of becoming a strong plant with bright green leaves.

One morning, rain fell gently on the garden. The water sank into the soil and reached the tiny seed. Soon, warm sunlight shone above the ground. The seed began to swell. A small root pushed downward, and a little shoot pushed upward.

Day by day, the shoot grew taller. It reached through the soil and finally saw the sunlight. A small pair of leaves opened. A butterfly landed nearby, and bees buzzed around the flowers in the garden.

The little plant kept growing with help from water, sunlight, air, and soil. Its stem became stronger, and more leaves appeared. After many days, a small yellow flower bloomed at the top.

The tiny seed had become a beautiful plant. In the garden, the new flower stood proudly among the taller plants. Its big dream had come true.
